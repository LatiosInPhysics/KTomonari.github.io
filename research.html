Introduction

Fundamentals　(Under construction)

Let us overview General Relativity, Metric-Affine Gravity, and Gauge Theories of Gravity, from the viewpoint of connections.
Suppose that \(M\) is a differentiable manifold, \(g\) is a metric tensor with a Lorentzian signature, and \(\nabla\) is an affine connection. We consider a structure \((M,g,\nabla)\).

General Relativity (Under construction)

General Relativity describes gravity as the curvature of spacetime, which is shaped by matter and energy.
The underlying geometry is equipped with the Levi-Civita connection, \(\overset{\circ}{\nabla}\), which satistfies the following two conditions:

\[ \overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{X}Y - \overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{Y}X = [X,Y] \tag{1} \]
and
\[ (Xg)(Y,Z) = g(\overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{X}Y,Z) + g(Y,\overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{X}Z) \,, \tag{2} \]
where \(X,Y,Z \in TM\). In a local coordinate system \(x^{\mu}\), the components of the Levi-Civita connection is given by
\[ \overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{\partial_{\mu}}\partial_{\nu} = \overset{\circ}{\Gamma}{}^{\rho}_{\mu\nu}\partial_{\rho} \,. \tag{3} \]
Explicitly, \(\overset{\circ}{\Gamma}{}^{\rho}_{\mu\nu}\) is expressed in
\[ \overset{\circ}{\Gamma}{}^{\rho}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{1}{2}g^{\rho\sigma}( \partial_{\mu}{g_{\nu\sigma}} + \partial_{\nu}{g_{\mu\sigma}} - \partial_{\sigma}{g_{\mu\nu}} ) \,, \tag{4} \]
where \(g_{\mu\nu}\) is the components of the metric tensor, \(g=g_{\mu\nu}dx^{\mu}\otimes dx^{\nu}\), and \(g^{\mu\nu}\) is the inverse components of \(g_{\mu\nu}\). Then, \(\overset{\circ}{\nabla}\) constitues the curvature tensor as follows:
\[ \overset{\circ}{R}(X,Y)Z = \overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{X}\overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{Y}Z - \overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{Y}\overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{X}Z + \overset{\circ}{\nabla}_{[X,Y]}Z \,. \tag{5} \]
In the local coordinates \(x^{\mu}\), the components of the curvature tensor is given by
\[ \overset{\circ}{R}{}^{\rho}{}_{\sigma\mu\nu} = \partial_{\mu}\overset{\circ}{\Gamma}{}^{\rho}_{\nu\sigma} - \partial_{\nu}\overset{\circ}{\Gamma}{}^{\rho}_{\mu\sigma} + \overset{\circ}{\Gamma}{}^{\rho}_{\mu\lambda}\overset{\circ}{\Gamma}{}^{\lambda}_{\nu\sigma} - \overset{\circ}{\Gamma}{}^{\rho}_{\nu\lambda}\overset{\circ}{\Gamma}{}^{\lambda}_{\mu\sigma} \,. \tag{6} \]
Contracting the first and the third indices, we obtain the Ricci tensor, \(\overset{\circ}{R}{}_{\mu\nu}=\overset{\circ}{R}{}^{\rho}{}_{\mu\rho\nu}\). Further contraction leads to the Ricci scalar, \(\overset{\circ}{R}=g^{\mu\nu}\overset{\circ}{R}{}_{\mu\nu}\).

Metric-Affine Gravity (Under construction)

Metric-affine gravity treats the spacetime metric and affine connection as independent geometric fields. This framework allows spacetime geometry to possess curvature, torsion, and non-metricity.

Gauge Theories of Gravity (Under construction)

Gauge theories of gravity formulate gravity using gauge fields associated with local spacetime symmetries, following the gauge principle familiar from particle physics.
