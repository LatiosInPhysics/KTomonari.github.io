Research Topics (Under construction)

General Relativity (Under construction)

General Relativity describes gravity as the curvature of spacetime, which is influenced by matter and energy.

Gauge Theories of Gravity (Under construction)

Gauge theories of gravity formulate gravity using gauge fields associated with local spacetime symmetries, following the gauge principle familiar from particle physics.

Metric-Affine Gravity (Under construction)

Metric-affine gravity treats the spacetime metric and affine connection as independent geometric fields. This framework allows spacetime geometry to possess curvature, torsion, and non-metricity.
