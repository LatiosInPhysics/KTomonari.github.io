Kyosuke Tomonari

Postdoctoral Researcher in Theoretical Physics

About Me

My research focuses on gravitational theory from the viewpoint of differential geometry. I also have experience working in the private sector as a consultant at a Big four firm, a systems engineer at an automotive company, and a production worker at a camera manufacturer. I love nature, mountains, lakes, the sea, forests, and the universe...

Research Interests

General Relativity; Metric-Affine Gravity; Gauge Theories of Gravity; Modified/Extended theories of graivty; Quantum gravity; Geometric method for physics; Artificial Intelligence (AI) for physics.
